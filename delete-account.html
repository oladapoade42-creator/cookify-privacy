Delete Your Cookify Account

You can ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to oladapomaxwell205@gmail.com with the subject "Cookify Account Deletion Request".
  2. Use the same email address you signed in with on Google, so we can verify the account is yours.
  3. We will confirm your request and complete the deletion, normally within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email, profile picture, and account ID)
  • Your username
  • Your comments and replies

What may be kept

We may keep limited information only where the law requires it or where it is needed to prevent fraud and abuse. Routine backups are cleared on their normal rotation schedule.

Want to remove just a comment? You can edit or delete your own comments directly inside the Cookify app without deleting your account.

Read how we handle your data in our Privacy Policy.
