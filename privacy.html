Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026 · Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cookify ("Cookify", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Cookify Android app (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information from Google Sign-In. Cookify uses Google Sign-In for authentication. When you sign in, Google shares the following with us:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile picture (if available)
  • A unique account identifier

We never receive or store your Google password.

b) Content you create. When you use the comments feature, we store:

  • Comments and replies you post
  • Edits you make to your comments
  • The username shown next to your comments
  • Timestamps of when content was posted or edited

c) Technical information. Like most apps, our service providers may automatically process limited technical data (such as IP address, device type, and app version) in order to deliver the service, keep it secure, and prevent abuse.

We do not intentionally collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in
  • To display your username and your comments and replies in the App
  • To let you edit and delete your own content
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the App
  • To detect and prevent spam, abuse, and violations of our rules
  • To respond to your support requests and comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Public Content and Usernames

Comments and replies you post, along with the username attached to them, are visible to other users of the App. Please do not post personal or sensitive information in comments. You can edit or delete your own comments at any time inside the App.

5. When We Share Information

We share personal information only:

  • With the service providers listed above, to operate the App
  • When required by law, legal process, or a valid government request
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of Cookify, our users, or the public
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of the App, in which case we will notify you of any change in how your data is handled

6. Data Retention

We keep your account information and content for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data and content within a reasonable period, normally within 30 days, except where we must keep certain information to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes. Backups are cleared on their normal rotation schedule.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and personal data
  • Withdraw consent and stop using the App at any time

To delete your account and data, follow the steps on our Delete Account page. For any other request, email us at the address in Section 11.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on our database. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Cookify is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process and store data in countries other than your own. By using the App, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations.
